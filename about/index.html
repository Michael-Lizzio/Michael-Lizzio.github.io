About

Software engineering student at RIT

I got a Raspberry Pi for Christmas in 2018 and I was super excited. I didn't really know what I was doing, so I just messed around with it and made silly projects, and some that were a lot more complicated than they needed to be. Looking back, I'm really glad I learned that way.

Since then I've built a Wordle solver, a cryptogram solver, a robot foosball goalie (with some help from my dad), and a lot more. Now I'm studying software engineering at RIT, and I spend my summers interning. The last two were at Greenman Pedersen, working on AI and GIS tools.

These days most of my free time goes into my own apps. Engine Miles and Burrow are on the App Store, and RelayReply is in a public beta. What I liked about that first Raspberry Pi is still what I like now: there's always something new to figure out.
