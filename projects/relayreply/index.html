RelayReply

Type what you need, and RelayReply makes the phone call, speaks for you, and asks you first when there's a decision to make.

AI-assisted
  • relayreply.com (public beta)
What it is
A web app that makes phone calls for you. You type what you need, RelayReply makes the call and speaks for you, and you read every word as a caption.
Why I made it
For a lot of people the phone call is the hardest part of getting something done, like people who are Deaf or hard of hearing, or who have trouble speaking on the phone.
What I did
I'm building all of it, using AI as a coding tool: the web app, the server that runs every call, and the part that turns each decision into a card for you.
How it turned out
It's in a public beta at relayreply.com, free to start with free practice calls.

The details

How a call works

You type who to call and what you need, like refilling a prescription. RelayReply places a real phone call and does the talking, and you read both sides as captions while it happens.

When there's a decision to make, like a 30-day or a 90-day supply, you get a card with the options. You tap one or type your own answer, and the AI says it for you.

Where it's at

RelayReply is in a public beta. It isn't a relay service (TRS or 711), and it can't call 911. More about it is on relayreply.com.
